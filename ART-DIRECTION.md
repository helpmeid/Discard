# Oakhaven — approved visual direction

The user liked the Oakhaven theme and asked for art that does not have a generic AI-generated appearance. The direction is restrained storybook fantasy: teal cloth and silver armor for the hero/father, ochre village sunlight, slate/ember armor and a burgundy cloak for the Duke, and a green misty watchtower for Garrow. Use clean designed silhouettes, economical texture, expressive faces, purposeful composition and a limited palette. Keep this continuity in future assets and UI.

The art was generated and then restyled with the built-in image generation tool. It is not claimed to be drawn by a human artist. The final atlas was visually inspected; four equal panels were extracted and JPEG-encoded for the runtime. These panels are embedded in `oakhaven-art.js` so the existing Discard loader includes and caches them without external image requests. The runtime asset is approximately 702 KB; its automated test caps it at 900 KB. Source atlas saved as the chat deliverable `Oakhaven-storybook-art.png`.

## Original generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: single cinematic storyboard atlas for an original fantasy action RPG called Oakhaven: Ash & Oath. Generate one landscape image divided into an EXACT 2 by 2 grid of FOUR equal rectangular cinematic paintings, no gutters, no borders, no text, no labels, no watermark. Each quadrant is a complete wide cinematic shot, with consistent character designs and beautifully polished painterly high-end game key art (not pixel art, not low-poly, not Minecraft). Top-left: peaceful medieval village Oakhaven at golden sunset, a 10-year-old dark-haired child and his father in a worn silver knight breastplate with a teal cloth tabard, father kneeling warmly beside the child, flowers and half-timber houses, emotional home. Top-right: same village burning at night, dramatic close foreground child peering from behind cellar wooden slats, frightened face lit by fire, outside silhouettes of original ash-armored marauders and orange flames, no gore. Bottom-left: low-angle close dramatic confrontation, father silver armor teal tabard bracing a battered round shield against the towering Obsidian Duke, original imposing charcoal plate armor with ember-red cracks and tattered burgundy cape, huge sword contacting shield and sparks bursting, ruined burning village, no gore. Bottom-right: eight years later, hero now 18 in silver armor with teal cape seen from behind, Old Man Garrow with gray beard and leather traveling coat at a ruined watchtower on a green misty hill, dawn sunbeams, hopeful epic composition. Rich painted detail, strong silhouettes, warm gold versus blue-green shadows, cinematic atmosphere. Keep important faces/action centrally within each separate quadrant so each quadrant can be cropped and displayed fullscreen as its own shot. This single atlas will be used directly in-game.

## Final restyling prompt

Edit target: this four-quadrant fantasy cinematic atlas. Preserve the EXACT 2 by 2 layout, story moments and original characters, but completely repaint in a restrained traditional gouache-and-ink storybook illustration style. Intentional graphic shapes, expressive believable faces, economical confident brushstrokes, subtle paper texture, limited forest green/ochre/slate/brick-red palette. Simplify armor and fabrics into clean designed shapes; no fiddly engraving, no tiny meaningless details. Make each quadrant feel like a carefully composed illustrated game story panel, with readable silhouettes, breathing room, hand-drawn line variation and purposeful asymmetry. Keep the child, father silver armor teal tabard, original dark-armored burgundy-caped Duke, gray-bearded Garrow and matching hero distinct and consistent across the shots. Eliminate the glossy digital fantasy-poster rendering, photoreal skin, excessive magical glow, excessive bloom and hyper-detailed surfaces. Anatomy and visible hands should be natural. No text, no labels, no signatures, no watermark. Four equal rectangular panels edge-to-edge with no gutters. This image will be cropped per quadrant as in-game cinematic scenes.
