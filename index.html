Discard is peer to peer: messages go straight between you and your friends. Both of you need to be online for a message to arrive; otherwise it waits and sends when you are both online. Your account and password are stored only on this computer. The password keeps others out of the app but does not encrypt your saved data, and it cannot be reset if you forget it.
